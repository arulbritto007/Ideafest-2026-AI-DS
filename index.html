About IDEAFEST 2026

IDEAFEST 2026 is organized by the ARINDASCI Association of the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Sir Isaac Newton College of Engineering and Technology.

The event aims to encourage innovation, interdisciplinary collaboration, technical knowledge sharing, and the practical application of emerging technologies.

It provides an opportunity for students to demonstrate their technical skills, present their projects, and explore solutions to real-world challenges.

Who Can Participate?

IDEAFEST 2026 welcomes motivated student innovators across diverse collegiate technical disciplines.

Engineering Colleges

Students enrolled in undergraduate and postgraduate programs across:

  • Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
  • Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AI&DS)
  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI&ML)
  • Agricultural Engineering
  • Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)
  • Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Information Technology (IT)
  • Civil Engineering
  • Biomedical Engineering
  • Cybersecurity
  • And other engineering disciplines

Polytechnic Colleges

Diploma engineering students pursuing studies in:

  • Computer Engineering
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
  • Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)
  • And other diploma disciplines

Arts and Science Colleges

Undergraduate and graduate students studying science and technology streams:

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Computer Science
  • Information Technology
  • Biotechnology
  • And other relevant disciplines

Important Eligibility Notice

External participation from other Engineering Colleges is included in the current permission letter. Participation by students from external Polytechnic and Arts & Science Colleges should be confirmed by the organizers before registration opens.

EVENT CRITERIA

Participation Guidelines

Please adhere to the following checklist to ensure a seamless expo presentation.

Domain Alignment

Projects must relate to at least one of the announced project domains.

Problem Statement & Proposed Solution

Participants should clearly explain the problem statement and proposed solution.

Technical Knowledge & Practical Application

Projects should demonstrate technical knowledge, creativity, and practical application.

Originality & Ethical Tech

Original work and responsible use of technology are encouraged.

Tools & Architecture Demonstration

Participants should explain the tools and technologies used in their projects.

Procedures & Regulations

Registration procedures, team size, submission deadlines, and other rules will be announced by the organizers.
